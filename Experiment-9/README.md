# Experiment 9

## Aim

To implement and evaluate efficient Bin Packing approximation algorithms (First Fit, First Fit Decreasing, and Best Fit Decreasing) and compare them to the theoretical lower bound.

## Algorithms

- First Fit (FF)
- First Fit Decreasing (FFD)
- Best Fit Decreasing (BFD)

## Result

The approximation algorithms were successfully implemented. For the given input list, the theoretical lower bound of bins required is 5, while all approximation heuristics (FF, FFD, BFD) achieved a packing of 6 bins.
